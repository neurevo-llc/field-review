About · Terms · Disclaimer

What this is, and what it isn't.

Neurevo is a forecasting company. We work in machine learning and neuroevolutionary methods, and our forecasting systems are measured every day against settled prices, by a method we publish in full. PHAROS is our flagship product: an advisory-only probabilistic forecasting and dispatch-planning service for battery storage in ERCOT, built for operators running energy arbitrage. Neurevo LLC is a small independent company, not a platform vendor.

What we do

We read public market data and issue a dispatch plan twice a day: which hours to charge, which to sell, and the spike forecast behind them. It is built to be one input among several for an operator running energy arbitrage. Each plan is built for one battery: its trading hub, its power and energy rating, and its duration.

What we don't do

  • We do not operate anything. There is no execution surface, no control pathway, and no arrangement under which PHAROS acts on a market or on a plant.
  • We do not connect to your systems. No hookup, no hardware, no network access, no plant telemetry. Our forecasts run on public data. Your own settlement data, which you already receive, is what the weekly and the fee are measured on.
  • We do not give investment or financial advice. Nothing we publish is a recommendation to buy, sell, or hold any security, commodity, or financial instrument, and we are not a licensed advisor of any kind.
  • We do not guarantee outcomes. Not accuracy, not revenue, not availability. See the disclaimer below, which is the operative statement.
  • We do not share your reports and schedules with anyone outside of your organization. Your BESS schedule is built specifically for your system, and it stays in house.

How we behave about evidence

Two commitments hold this together and are worth more than any claim we could make about ourselves. First, we published the evaluation we lost, in full, with the protocol fingerprinted before we looked at the data. Second, we run a graded record that accrues day by day and counts the misses next to the hits, on a method we publish in full. If we ever stop doing either of those things, treat everything else on this site with suspicion.

Privacy

This site carries no analytics, no advertising trackers, no third-party scripts and no cookies set by us. Nothing on these pages, fonts included, loads from another company's servers. If you send us an inquiry, we use what you send to prepare your read and to reply to you, and for nothing else — we don't sell it and we don't add you to a list. Ask and we'll delete it.
