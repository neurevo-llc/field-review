Who this is for

Merchant battery storage in ERCOT, earning on the spread.

If you own or run a BESS in ERCOT and your revenue depends on when you charge and when you discharge, you are the whole of our audience. Everything here is built around that one decision.

Ancillary services carried this fleet for years. They are getting crowded, the clearing prices show it, and the money is moving back toward the spread. That is the market this service was built for, and more of the fleet walks into it every quarter.

  • You dispatch it yourself. No trading desk, or a small one, and the call still lands on your team.
  • Arbitrage is where your money comes from. Charging cheap and discharging into the expensive hours is the business, not a sideline.
  • Your hub and your hardware, not a class average. The plan is priced at your trading hub, the one on your order form, and built for your power and energy rating and the duration you actually run.
  • If ancillary services pay most of your bills, we are probably not for you yet. A better price forecast will not move your revenue much, and we would rather say so than sell you something. That is worth revisiting as AS gets more crowded.
  • If your battery carries ancillary service some of the time, those hours are paused. No fee is taken on them, no Baseline credit is given, and everything else is measured on the capacity you traded.

The record

We published the evaluation we lost.

You can re-run a backtest until it flatters you. So we locked the protocol, fingerprinted it before we looked at the data, and ran it once. We lost. That result sits at the top of the Trust page, where it belongs. What survived it is what we sell.

How the record is kept

  • Written down before the hours it plans. Every issue, morning and mid-day, is archived and fingerprinted when it is issued, before the first hour it plans.
  • Graded the next day on settled prices. ERCOT's own settlement, not our replay of it, and not a window we picked afterwards.
  • The misses are counted with the hits. Both failure modes: the spikes we do not call, and the spikes forecast on a day that stayed quiet.
  • The thresholds were frozen before the record started. Every cutoff was frozen before the record it grades began: the first in the sealed evaluation, and each later forecast's in its own sealed protocol before its first issue. None is re-tuned as the days accrue.
  • Every amendment is dated and logged. The protocol can be changed in the open. It cannot be changed quietly. The pre-registration's amendment ledger is on the Trust page, and each later sealed protocol keeps its own.

That is the method the live record is kept by, and the method is what decides whether a record means anything. We open the record itself on a live screen when we meet.
