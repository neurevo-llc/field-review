Get started

Start on your own asset.

Tell us which resource you run and we set up a virtual walkthrough. Before we meet, we measure it from public ERCOT data: what it earned, against what the same battery could have earned on the same days. On the call we walk you through the product on a demonstration account: both daily briefs, the live dashboard, the weekly and Quarterly Reports, and the graded record. Your own asset goes on the order form, and its plans are built for it from the first day of service.

What you get

A virtual walkthrough of the product as a client sees it, on a demonstration account. And a short written read on one resource: how it behaved over a window ending about sixty days ago, the most recent ERCOT has published, where the money was on the days that paid, and how big the difference was. Hours the battery held ancillary service are set aside, so the read measures only what was traded. Measured, not guessed. The telemetry and the prices are both public, so you can check our arithmetic.

It costs nothing and commits you to nothing. If the number turns out to be small, we will say so.

What we need from you

The SCED resource name, the identifier ERCOT publishes your resource under, and where to reach you. We work from public records, so we do not need access to anything of yours.

One resource, one read

If you run several, name the one you are most curious about and mention the rest in the note. We would rather do one carefully than four quickly.

Inquiry

The resource identifier as it appears in ERCOT's public records.
