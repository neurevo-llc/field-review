Each morning's screen is filed once, never overwritten and never pruned. That is what makes it a record rather than a view.

Two issues a day

You get two issues a day, and they carry the same weight. The 06:00 issue plans the day. The 13:45 issue re-plans from 15:00 with its own forecast and takes over wherever the two overlap; the next morning’s issue takes over again from 07:00. The briefs stay exactly as issued; the dashboard always shows the newest plan of record.
