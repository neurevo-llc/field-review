  • A late or missing brief: the whole day is not billed Thursday · 2026-10-01posted 20:20 CT Rule

    From 1 October 2026 a day with a late or missing brief is not billed at all; whatever the plans gained that day is yours.

    A brief is on time when it reaches your shelf within an hour of its scheduled issue: the morning brief by 07:00 CT, the mid-day brief by 14:45 CT. If either of an operating day's two briefs is later than that, or never arrives, no fee is charged for that whole operating day. The day stays in your weekly report, marked, and its plan still counts in the performance figures.

    This replaces the earlier rule, under which only the hours the late brief governed went unbilled.
