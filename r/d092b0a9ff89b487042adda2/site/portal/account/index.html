An owner sees everything, including Reports and the fee line inside them. An operator sees the dispatch briefs and the dashboard, and not Reports. A change applies from the person's next click. Adding someone sends them a sign-in link; removing them keeps the record of their seat and of everything it opened. The founding owner's seat is permanent — it cannot be removed or changed here by anyone; if the owner changes, let us know and we will handle it. Every seat is a named individual — there is no shared company login — and each change here is recorded in the access log as yours.
